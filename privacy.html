Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

CAST is a fishing companion app. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and who we share it with. We have tried to keep it specific rather than generic — if something here is unclear, contact us and we will explain it.

1. What we collect

Account details. When you create an account we store your name, email address and a securely hashed password. We never store your password in readable form.

Your fishing data. Your catches, sessions, notes and photos are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers unless you explicitly share them (for example, sharing a catch card or joining a live session with a friend).

Location. If you grant permission, we use your device location to show nearby fishing spots and fetch local weather, tide and solunar conditions. Your coordinates are sent to our weather providers to retrieve a forecast. We do not store a history of your movements.

Photos you scan. If you use the AI fish identifier, the photo you capture is sent to our processing service to identify the species and returned to you. Photos are not used to build advertising profiles.

2. Why we collect it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To show fishing spots, live weather, tides and solunar windows relevant to where you are.
  • To identify fish species from a photo when you ask us to.
  • To let you fish "live" with friends you have invited, and to show their session activity to you and yours to them.
  • To process a subscription payment, if you choose to buy one.

3. Services we use

We share the minimum necessary data with these providers so the app can work:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, and the database that holds your account.
  • Open-Meteo — weather and marine forecasts (receives coordinates, not your identity).
  • Anthropic — powers the AI fish identifier and advisor (receives the photo or question you submit).
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons — openly licensed species photographs.
  • Global Fishing Watch — public vessel activity data for marine spots.
  • Stripe — payment processing on the web. Card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store them.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. Fishing spot data

CAST includes named water features derived from Overture Maps and OpenStreetMap, which are openly licensed. A named water feature is not proof of public fishing access — always confirm licences, permissions and local rules yourself.

5. Your choices and rights

Delete your account at any time. Open Settings → Delete Account in the app. This permanently deletes your account and the data associated with it on our servers, and clears your data from the device. It cannot be undone.

Location. You can refuse or revoke location permission in your device settings at any time; the app still works, with fewer location-aware features.

Access and correction. You can view and edit your profile in the app, or contact us to request a copy of the account data we hold.

6. Children

CAST is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Security and retention

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. We keep your account data for as long as your account exists; when you delete your account it is removed.

8. Changes

If we materially change this policy we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
